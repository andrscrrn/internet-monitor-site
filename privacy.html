Privacy Policy

Internet Monitor for Mac · Effective October 2, 2026

Summary

Internet Monitor collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you do in the app, and nothing the app measures, ever leaves your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

The app stores its measurements (connection checks, outages, speed test results) and your preferences as plain files on your Mac, inside the app's own sandboxed container. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network connections the app makes

Internet Monitor's job is to measure your connection, which requires talking to the internet. This is the complete list of connections, none of which carry any personal data:

  • Reachability probes to public DNS servers (1.1.1.1, 8.8.8.8, 9.9.9.9): brief connection handshakes used to measure latency and detect outages.
  • A DNS resolution check (resolving google.com) to detect DNS failures.
  • A public IP lookup via api.ipify.org, so the app can show you your public IP address. The response is displayed locally and nothing beyond the standard request is sent.

Permissions

The app may ask for permission to send notifications (outage and recovery alerts). Saving a report or log export writes only to the location you choose in the save dialog.

If you opt into showing Wi-Fi network names, the app asks for Location permission. This is an Apple requirement: macOS treats the Wi-Fi name (SSID) as location-sensitive data. Internet Monitor uses the permission solely to read that name and label measurements with the network they ran on; your location is never computed, stored, or sent anywhere.

App Privacy label

On the Mac App Store, this app's privacy label is Data Not Collected.
